# ERP 0.12.2 — Dedicated economic mapping of rolling equity factors

## Scope and rationale
Preserve v0.12.1, including the six previously specified rolling FMPs (RP0 and RP12, three PCs each), fixed-formation pricing, JK data-safety parsing, and Rates. No change to weights, selection, sign conventions or earlier results. The old mapping used a December 2005 cutoff even though rolling returns begin January 2006. The legacy FF-only shock study remains available; it is not directly comparable to the new common sample.

## New experiment
- Formation January 2006–December 2011; validation January 2012 onward (actual coverage is printed and exported). The extra year ensures at least 60 valid training months can remain after known JK gaps. Require 36 validation months. No backfill of RP returns, no zero imputation. Common calendar intersects all FF/RP/holdout returns and both current and preceding calendar-month shocks. Missing current or previous shock excludes the affected observation from every specification. `calendar.csv` records this.
- All FF and RP: contemporaneous shock exposure, HAC t/p and OOS R² versus training mean; lag-one prediction. Contemporaneous fitted values are not forecasts.
- For each RP: FF-controlled shock regression and OOS attribution increment versus FF only; compare training alpha and ex-post OOS alpha with and without shock controls. Ex-post OOS regression is descriptive, not fitted before the OOS observations. These are conditional associations, not causal evidence or pricing proof.
- Pricing horse race on identical 50 holdout portfolio definitions and dates: FF; FF+JK; FF+RP0; FF+RP0+JK; FF+RP12; FF+RP12+JK. Do not stack RP0 and RP12. Fixed formation FMB and two-step shrinkage GMM, using actual rolling FMP returns in both periods. Positive RMSE improvement means a lower annualized mean-pricing RMSE than the matched parent. Paired circular block bootstrap (399 draws, block 12 complete-case observations), conditional on fitted training parameters, resamples common OOS mean returns and recomputes the difference of RMSEs. This is not an inference procedure for monthly return forecasts or unconditional full-model uncertainty.
- Economic premium diagnostic: fit a shock-only linear SDF to training holdout excess returns, using standardized centered shocks and minimum-norm least squares. Map covariance with FF and RP returns to shock-implied expected returns, with component contributions and realized means. These are model-implied, not structurally identified causal premium shares. No assertion that each individual contribution is statistically significant. Correlated shock attribution depends on representation.
- Repeat the dedicated experiment separately for available FRED changes (label FRED_PROXY); do not conflate these with JK announcement shocks. CPI release consensus remains optional and not supplied.

No external download was tested here.

## Interpretation and limitations
The 2012+ period has already appeared in previous analyses: this is a retrospective extension, NOT fresh independent confirmation of RP12_PC2. No parameter search or change to the selected RP factors. FMB lacks Shanken/weak-identification corrections; GMM shrinkage invalidates conventional J-test; HAC and block resampling compress excluded calendar months. Holdout portfolio definitions may share constituents with discovery portfolios. Bootstrap intervals do not include parameter estimation or multiple testing uncertainty. No trading costs or structural macro identification added here. Real market results still require running the notebook with source files.
