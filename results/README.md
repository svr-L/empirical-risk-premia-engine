# Empirical evidence

The release preserves three completed historical notebooks rather than claiming a new market-data run. The current notebooks in `../notebooks/` have no outputs and use the consolidated source. The historical notebooks in `evidence/` retain their original embedded source, printed results and tables; transient Colab metadata and upload widgets are removed. `evidence/manifest.json` records original and published SHA-256 hashes. See `tables/provenance.csv` for the zero-based cell and output index of every extracted table. The table containing the joint cross-asset decomposition uses annualized column labels because the source display explicitly multiplies monthly means by 12; the cost-sensitivity table labels its model index and cost columns explicitly.

| Notebook | Empirical coverage | Use |
|---|---|---|
| [Equity v0.13](evidence/equity_v013_executed.ipynb) | 1963–Aug 2026 data; main dynamic pricing Jan 2012–Aug 2026 | Main Equity and cross-asset claims |
| [Rates v0.2.5](evidence/rates_v025_executed.ipynb) | Curve through Oct 2026 after cutoff; 3/6/12M forecasts evaluated on model-specific dates | Main Rates forecasting and rotation claims |
| [Equity standard benchmarks](evidence/equity_standard_executed.ipynb) | Jul 1963–Aug 2026, rolling attribution Jul 1968–Aug 2026 | FF/AQR benchmark comparisons |

Run `python scripts/build_results.py` at the repository root to regenerate the CSV tables and four figures from these preserved outputs (no network). Values reflect displayed precision; these are **summary tables**, not a substitute for raw forecast panels. Full model-generated `dynamic_all_bootstrap.csv`, `dynamic_all_factor_scores.csv`, raw forecasts and provider snapshots were not supplied. They are generated by the research notebook and should be saved in a subsequent run, subject to provider data terms. Consequently, we do not claim statistical significance of the headline pricing improvements or incremental monthly timing skill. The current research uses revised historical data.
