# ERP 0.13 — Dynamic risk-price estimation and cross-asset decomposition

## Scope
Additive research release from 0.12.2. Original FF/AQR, legacy RP0/RP12, macro/JK experiments and Rates are retained. No change to the old portfolio weights. The historical v0.13 Equity run is included under `results/evidence/`; this document describes the implementation and not a fresh run. Historical periods are retrospective and already explored, not fresh independent confirmation of RP12_PC2.

## Four interventions

### 1. Dynamic mean pricing
For each separate panel, intersect FF, realized RP, discovery and holdout returns and any extra factors. Use common calendar months for fixed, expanding and trailing 72-calendar-month pricing windows (at least 60 complete observations). First forecast only after sufficient rolling formation. Refit every 3 observed months, use coefficients and mean estimates only from earlier observations. Fixed benchmark uses the first common dynamic formation date; not identical to the earlier dedicated 2011 experiment when missing data shift the first date. Predict the next monthly excess return using expected-return estimates rather than realized factor returns. No realized target-month driver enters the prediction. This is dynamic historical moment estimation, not yet a conditional state-space model of risk prices. Compare against simple historical asset means. Separate annualized cross-sectional RMSE of mean forecast errors from monthly prediction RMSE. Bootstrap paired OOS paths for model additions, dynamic vs fixed, shrinkage vs plain and historical-mean benchmark. Conditional on estimated forecast paths; does not include full estimation uncertainty, weak-factor corrections or multiple testing adjustment. Calendar gaps are recorded; HAC and block resampling use complete observations rather than exact month intervals.

### 2. Gaussian shrinkage
FMB: estimate betas on formation, run monthly cross-sectional regressions with intercept, estimate mean price vector and its HAC covariance V. Prior mean zero, prior covariance D diagonal, monthly factor price SD equal to 0.25 times formation factor volatility; intercept weak prior SD 0.10. Posterior mean D(D+V)^(-1) lambda_hat and covariance D-D(D+V)^(-1)D; plug-in empirical covariance, not full Bayesian estimation of beta uncertainty. No shrinkage parameters selected on evaluation data. Direct mean and direct shrinkage are separate estimates of traded returns (FF/AQR, FMP, and traded extra proxies); FMB/GMM/GX prices are not assumed to equal traded expected returns. Two-step shrinkage GMM normalizes centered factors, uses regularized HAC weighting and maps SDF coefficients back into factor price units; no standard J-test claimed.

### 3. RP-PCA and Giglio–Xiu
Lettau–Pelger balanced-panel moment matrix R'R/T + gamma * mean(R)mean(R)'. Gamma=-1 corresponds to centered PCA; gamma=11 corresponds to weight 12 on mean term. Restrict to the null space of [ones, FF betas]' BEFORE the eigendecomposition. Return portfolios are gross-one, dollar and formation-beta neutral. Annual refit on trailing 120 months; Hungarian matching by absolute overlap of new and previous unit eigenvectors, sign anchored to previous formation. New CRP factors do not replace legacy RP factors. HAC alpha and Holm on six new candidates; broader historical research multiplicity remains.

Three-pass estimator: centered PCA on discovery returns with normalized latent scores, cross-section without intercept on formation holdout assets, projection of observed factors on latent scores. Report observed-factor prices and projection R2; separately report latent-model pricing. Bootstrap all three passes on synchronized 12-observation circular blocks for k=(5,8,12) at initial 2006–2011 formation. Dynamic point estimates use k=8, including projections of extra risks if available. Exploratory bootstrap intervals are NOT published weak-factor-robust asymptotic inference. References: Lettau & Pelger (2020), https://www.nber.org/papers/w24618 ; Giglio & Xiu (2021), https://doi.org/10.1086/714090 .

### 4. Cross-asset returns and conditional interactions
- FUNDED_RATES: actual ERP Level/Slope/Curvature funded factor returns (same adapter as Rates v0.9). Load original Kim–Wright curve and FEDFUNDS or an exported ERP factor_returns.csv. No par yield substitution. Fitted curve is revised history.
- ETF_PROXY: adjusted USD prices via optional yfinance download or local CSV. IEF minus RF, HYG minus IEF (not duration-neutral credit), UUP minus RF (USD futures fund), DBC minus RF (commodity futures fund), VIXY minus RF (volatility futures fund, not variance risk premium). No pre-inception backfill, no missing-return fill, no use of unadjusted prices. Missing end-of-month price fails coverage; require last quote within 7 calendar days of month end. JOINT_XA uses funded Rates and other ETF proxies, excluding duplicate IEF duration. Sources and coverage exported. These are traded exposures/proxies, not proofs of economic risk premia. No external downloads verified in this runtime.
- JK and FRED_PROXY: separate nontraded shock/changes panels, not treated as return forecasts. MANAGED: MKT and HML times two predeclared lagged macro state variables (inflation and term spread); lag 2 calendar months, trailing standardization on past 60 months (minimum 36), clip [-2,2]; revised historical states not release-vintage data.
- Extra factors in pricing are hedged against FF with formation betas, retaining their mean/intercept. Actual hedged traded return depends on the scheme, so do not compare those target returns across schemes without harmonizing hedges. For traded cross-asset economic decomposition use raw drivers, fit FF/RP on them, export contribution beta_j * mean(driver_j) and retained intercept. Mean projection is not causal decomposition; correlated driver allocation depends on basis. Also export separate contemporaneous fitted returns, not forecasts.

## Notebook / data
Run `notebooks/01_equity_factor_discovery_and_pricing.ipynb` from top after installation. The v0.12.2 FRED local-file validation and upload fallback remain. JK author data remain. Extra panels have explicit status outputs. If yfinance is unavailable or blocked, specify ETF_ADJUSTED_CSV (CSV Date,IEF,HYG,UUP,DBC,VIXY containing adjusted USD closes); if original Rates acquisition blocked, specify RATES_FACTORS_CSV, or RATES_CURVE_PATH and RATES_FUNDING_PATH. These files are optional; the core Equity experiment does not require them. No synthetic data fallback. Each panel has its own common dates; compare scores only within panel. Exports dynamic_<panel>/{forecasts,prices,scores,factor_scores,bootstrap,gx,calendar}.csv and manifest.json, combined score files, source status, CRP weights/diagnostics/premia, initial GX bootstrap, raw traded economic contributions. Large CSVs expected. No portfolio allocation or new trading-cost model included.

`hedges.csv` additionally records formation FF hedge betas and maximum absolute training correlation for each added driver. Neutrality is a training property, not guaranteed after formation; overlapping exposure to RP or other extra drivers remains a joint pricing/identification question.
